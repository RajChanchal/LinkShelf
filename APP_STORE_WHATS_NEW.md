# LinkShelf 1.5 — What’s New

## English (en)

- LinkShelf now keeps your links in a new, more robust database. Links from earlier versions move over automatically the first time you open the app, and a backup of your original data stays on your Mac.
- Sharing a link from Safari or another app now confirms only after the link is saved, and shows a clear message if it couldn't be.
- Clearer messages if your links can't be loaded or saved.
- Website icons are now kept in a separate cache.
